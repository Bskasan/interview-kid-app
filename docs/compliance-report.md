## 2. Repo shareability & private material (A4 — the decision of this round)

Verified with `git ls-files`: **PROJECT-DESCRIPTION.md (the evaluator's own brief), CLAUDE.md
(AI-assistant instructions), AGENTS.md, all of `docs/` (61 files: 53 ADRs, 7 feature docs,
this report), and `.claude/settings.json` are tracked and pushed** — `.gitignore` excludes
none of them. Git history also references them (commit messages like "docs: record the
cleanup policy (ADR 0053)"), so history and files are entangled.

The brief allows AI tools and grades decision ownership, so nothing here is against the
rules — this is purely a question of what you _want_ evaluators to see.

| Option                                                                                                                                                              | What it means                                                                                                                                                               | Cost / risk                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **(a) Share as-is** (private repo, evaluators as collaborators)                                                                                                     | They see the full decision record — 53 ADRs directly evidence "defend every decision"; they also see CLAUDE.md (your AI working instructions) and their own brief re-hosted | Zero effort. The AI-workflow depth becomes part of your story — defensible ("kararların sahibi sizsiniz"), but it is a story you must own in the interview                                                         |
| **(b) Untrack process files now** (`git rm --cached` CLAUDE.md, PROJECT-DESCRIPTION.md, AGENTS.md, `.claude/`, optionally `docs/`; add to `.gitignore`; one commit) | Files leave HEAD; evaluators browsing the repo see only product + README                                                                                                    | S effort, **but files remain in history** — anyone reading commits still finds them; commit messages still cite ADRs. Half-measure unless combined with (c)                                                        |
| **(c) Clean-history export** (fresh repo from a squashed or filtered copy; share that link)                                                                         | Complete separation of product from process                                                                                                                                 | M–L effort; loses the granular conventional-commit history ("git history is part of what gets evaluated") or requires careful `git filter-repo` surgery with renumbered risks; existing remote/PR history diverges |
| **(d) Keep `docs/` tracked, untrack only CLAUDE.md + PROJECT-DESCRIPTION.md + AGENTS.md + `.claude/`**                                                              | ADRs stay as evaluation evidence; the assistant-instruction files and the re-hosted brief leave HEAD                                                                        | S effort; same history caveat as (b), but the _interesting_ files for an evaluator (ADRs) stay, and the leftover history traces are mundane                                                                        |

Recommendation: **(a) or (d)**. (a) if you are comfortable presenting the AI-assisted
process as your documented working method; (d) if you prefer the repo to lead with product +
ADRs while accepting that history remains discoverable. (b) alone buys little; (c) only if
you decide the process must be genuinely invisible.
